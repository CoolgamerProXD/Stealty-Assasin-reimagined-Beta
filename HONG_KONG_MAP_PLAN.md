# Hong Kong campaign — production map plan

**Current implementation:** the three required city missions now have separate campaign entries and checkpoints but reuse one connected 90 × 90 m city scene with original 70 × 70 m district extensions; they are still blockout-scale and need more architectural distinction, route depth, vertical interiors, population routines, density and performance work. The required campaign order is Initiation → Tung Chung/Key 01 → Streets/Key 02 → Commercial District/Key 03 → Harbour/Key 04 → Ngong Ping/Key 05 → House/70-digit code → USB decryption → betrayal. Campaign schema 2 stores exact completed target IDs independently from keys and safe code; schema 3 adds the saved bus-stop completion and player-selected callsign. Tung Chung now requires the USB robber and Constance’s operative as two targets; the train courier is a different character. The House/code gate and post-decryption scene are covered by Godot campaign progression checks and Windows exported-scene smoke in run [45201173194](https://github.com/CoolgamerProXD/Stealty-Assasin-reimagined-Beta/actions/runs/45201173194). The smoke used a fresh save, so the post-USB scene followed its incomplete-campaign redirect rather than exercising the valid decryption-to-rescue path; this full sequence still needs a seeded-state runtime test and human playthrough. A new interactive, non-graphic bus-stop opener now prompts a player-selected 1–24 character callsign, stages the trip/evade/restrain beats, and leads to the Initiation briefing. The callsign/completion flag are persisted in campaign schema 3, and the opening grants no mission target or USB key. This new flow awaits Godot CI verification. Final environment quality, cultural review, and new Assignment 008 image evidence remain outstanding. Meridian remains a late optional replay.

The following sections are **historical design intentions**, not claims that every named sequence or feature was implemented.

## Canon opening / before Initiation
At a fictional bus stop, Ruby chases a hooded robber who stole a sealed USB. The new interactive, non-graphic scene asks the player to choose a callsign, trip the robber with E, evade his swing with A/D, and restrain him with E. Ruby then reveals herself, says she personally recommended the agent to BLACKLINE, and sends them to the Initiation briefing. This is a prompted staged scene, not simulated combat or a fully navigable street; it does not grant a target completion or USB key. Keep the USB contents and the agent's personal history unknown.

## 01 / Initiation
Keep the evaluation annex, its two guards, three records and moving unit. Ruby remains the handler and the introduction remains skippable.

## 02 / Tung Chung — intended playable vertical slice
**Spatial plan:** a broad concourse is the central landmark and orientation hub. One side has ticketing and entry, the opposite side has a small row of shops and facilities. Two pedestrian connections lead to a waiting area and a fictional platform, giving a loop around a central information island. An upper walkway and decorative escalators can establish scale without suggesting real routes or access. The platform leads to a self-contained train interior; no railway access gameplay. Use original bilingual-inspired graphic language, not copied commercial signage. Separate public and staff spaces are legible through color and signage, not a copy of the real station.

**Population:** budget around 12–18 visible characters for the first slice, distributed between circulation, ticketing, shops and waiting. Use several short, deterministic walking/idle routes with offset start times and distinct clothing; avoid spawning an uncontrolled crowd. One Robber moves between ticketing and the information island, visibly distinctive, and is the first identification/objective. The second target, Constance's operative, waits near the shops and moves towards the platform when approached. The target should recognize the player and trigger a staged escape, rather than teleport without feedback. Exact names and deeper lore remain open.

**Story flow:** complete the distinct Robber and Constance’s operative objectives by explicit non-graphic assessment tags → physically recover Key 01 as a separate action → follow the separate train courier through the fictional, connected carriages and extract. Dialogue provides intelligence only; neither dialogue nor ordinary incapacitation can satisfy either objective or grant the key. Persist both target IDs and the physical key independently. Use fictional scene transitions for the train; never teach physical rail access or stunts. A target's completed state is distinct from temporary incapacitation.

**Discoveries:** a visible-but-unlabelled maintenance pouch can reveal an abstract wrench; a discovered station display opportunity can require it and briefly change a scripted crowd route. An unrelated notice provides optional contextual intel. Instinct only highlights known, nearby items; target outlines require identification/discovery. Do not simulate real-world sabotage. Place different optional paths on either side of the information island, with obvious landmarks to avoid navigation confusion.

**Performance:** modular geometry with shared materials/instancing, a limited pool of animated NPCs, throttled perception, shared navmesh and no expensive real-time reflections. LOW reduces render scale/decorations. Validate actual routes, saves, combat and camera and measure on target hardware before calling it complete.

## 03 / Hong Kong — intended city beyond the station
**Plan:** a dense, connected district rather than an empty expanse. Station forecourt is the center; a commercial street runs east to a restaurant court, a quieter residential lane loops north, and a pedestrian walkway returns to the forecourt above a small public square. Multiple connections allow a loop, not disconnected rooms. Shops, food stalls, apartments and public seating supply recognizable navigation landmarks. Keep the scope to meaningful blocks with occluded sight lines, not geographic fidelity.

**Population zones:** commuters at the forecourt, diners at restaurant court, workers at commercial entries and residents in the lane. Short route groups vary idle and walking activities. Ruby briefs from BLACKLINE; optional contacts do not grant target completion or keys. Optional intel in a shop and a public-square discovery provide reasons to explore. Unknown clients, deal terms and USB contents stay unresolved; no new Covenant head identities are introduced. Discoverable tools can gate fictional, reversible opportunity interactions and alternate routes; no realistic hazard systems.

**Performance:** stream or activate districts in bounded groups, use shared meshes/materials and limit visible agent count. The currently available city is an early blockout; the planned detailed districts and target-hardware benchmarks are not complete.

## 04 / Ngong Ping — environment and target design only
A fictional cable-car arrival frames mountain ridges and gives an uninterrupted view toward a respectful, recognizable Tian Tan Buddha landmark. A village-inspired plaza has timber storefronts and visitor seating; a garden path and a temple-inspired forecourt lead toward a distant elevated viewing area. The Buddha and religious spaces are landmarks, never targets or destructible objects. Tourists photograph the view, visitors walk between plaza and garden, and staff maintain public areas. Use stylized layered mountain silhouettes, vegetation clusters and atmospheric depth rather than claiming geographic or architectural exactness. Cable-car travel is a non-interactive cinematic vista; no operational details.

**Physical target concept:** an as-yet unnamed visitor in a distinctive muted teal jacket, pale scarf and dark trousers, carrying a small sketchbook. Their presentation suggests an observant, unhurried personality: they alternate between studying the view near the Buddha, sitting to sketch and walking to the forecourt. This is a visual/schedule design only; no backstory, Constance connection or deeper lore is established. Allow identification by observation, not an omniscient marker. Respectful public-space behavior is essential.

**Status:** the Ngong Ping exploration blockout exists, with the target and interconnected sublevels now present in the campaign. This remains a stylized approximation; obtain cultural and location review before treating the landmark depiction as final. The Buddha is never a target or destructible object. The Big Buddha and site's religious significance must be represented respectfully.

## 05 / The House — short recovery operation
A compact fictional safe house follows Ngong Ping and requires all five physical keys. Two local guards are security obstacles, not new mandatory targets. The player enters through the front, reaches the back-room wall safe, physically opens it for the independent 70-digit code, then exits to the street. Keep the route short (about five minutes), with no additional target or lore reveal. The current original House block is under `scripts/world/house_mission_map.gd`; House target/key/code progression is covered by the passing integrated campaign checks in run [45201173194](https://github.com/CoolgamerProXD/Stealty-Assasin-reimagined-Beta/actions/runs/45201173194). Scene smoke is not a visual acceptance or full human route.

## USB decryption and betrayal canon
Five keys unlock the encryption, but the 70-digit safe code is separate. Decryption follows House and must not reveal the unresolved USB contents, deal or client. After decryption Ruby briefs the unnamed senior BLACKLINE manager; the agent eavesdrops, the manager turns on Ruby, and the agent intervenes. The senior manager is secretly a Covenant spy. Only Ruby and the agent know, and they begin a secret hunt. The Covenant has ten heads; only Constance is named, with her Tung Chung operative involved. The businessman’s Prometheus project is discreetly funded by the Covenant and exchanges findings/requests; it is not Covenant headquarters, and its research purpose remains unknown. The scene is implemented and the progression state checks pass in run [45201173194](https://github.com/CoolgamerProXD/Stealty-Assasin-reimagined-Beta/actions/runs/45201173194), but the Windows smoke launches it with a fresh save that fails its post-decryption guard and redirects to menu. Seeded valid-state runtime and interactive rescue testing remain outstanding.
