# PROJECT ASSASSIN

An offline Godot 4.4.1 stealth-sandbox prototype. BLACKLINE is the player's employer; Ruby is the handler and the Covenant is a separate organization. Playable assignments include **Initiation**, six required Hong Kong operations, and an optional late replay of **The Meridian**. Independent map studies remain under Exploration Previews.

## Play

Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.4.1 and press F5. Choose Campaign or a mission from Mission Selection. Ruby's briefing identifies the target; the mission HUD tracks the target, evidence, awareness, equipment and interaction prompts.

- **Initiation:** disable the moving assessment unit (E nearby or a well-aimed training pulse), collect all three records and reach the exit. Two guards patrol. Completion unlocks Quiet Line, then starts the skippable Ruby meeting. Optional Meridian is placed after the required Hong Kong story rather than ahead of Tung Chung.
- **The Meridian:** explore the original hotel and connected west wing, including the upper guest rooms. Follow the ramp in the west wing to the private suite. Confront or tag primary target **Adrian Locke**, the operations director who authorized the archive transfer. Question records employee **Ivo Chen** as a separate witness, collect any two of the three records and reach the exit. The uniform grants staff access, not secure access.
- **Equipment:** press 1 for the unsuppressed, five-charge fictional training sidearm. Hold **right mouse** to aim, then press **left mouse** to fire an instantaneous pulse along the centre crosshair. It is blocked by walls, disables the unit and temporarily incapacitates people; nearby NPCs react to the noise. After Initiation, choose Quiet Line in Loadout before deploying to Meridian. Press 2 to equip it and Q to temporarily incapacitate a nearby unobstructed NPC. Quiet Line is not permitted in Initiation; the sidearm is always issued. A successful pulse on Locke completes the primary objective and leaves him permanently incapacitated for that checkpoint; Quiet Line only incapacitates him temporarily. A nonviolent confrontation completes his objective without a downed pose.
- Hold **left Ctrl** for Instinct: the briefing-identified primary target appears as a red character silhouette through solid geometry; nearby visible, unobstructed intel and the uniform appear yellow (default range four metres). Ordinary NPCs and undiscovered items are not revealed. WASD moves, mouse looks, Shift runs, C crouches, E interacts, F5 saves, F9 loads and Escape pauses. The Ruby scene can be skipped with Escape or its button. Actual cover can block an NPC's sight of a crouched player; crouching has no automatic visibility bonus. NPCs can notice visible incapacitated people and investigate locally.

Settings offer persisted LOW, MEDIUM and HIGH rendering scales (70%, 85%, 100%), shadow toggles/atlas sizes, optional practical lights, HIGH fine decoration and FXAA, and an FPS-counter toggle. The experimental lobby probe was removed after it made HIGH unacceptably dark.

Campaign and settings have separate versioned saves. Campaign version 2 persists exact completed target IDs separately from mission completion and recovered physical keys; legacy target-operation completion flags are not trusted when they cannot prove the current objective requirements. Meridian retains `user://meridian_save.json`; Initiation uses `user://initiation_save.json`. Per-mission checkpoints retain player position, equipment, target completion and remaining charges. NPC schedules, suspicion and temporary incapacitation are transient; target completion cannot be inferred from dialogue or ordinary incapacitation.

## Hong Kong operations

After Initiation, deploy in order to **Tung Chung → The Streets → The District → The Harbour → Ngong Ping → The House**. Meridian is an optional replay after the required story, not an interruption. Tung Chung requires separate permanent assessment-tag completions for the USB robber and Constance’s operative, then a physical Key 01 pickup; the train courier is a different person. Streets, Commercial District, and Harbour are separate missions with their own target, physical key, and extraction: the market vendor/Key 02, corporate fixer/Key 03, and wealthy harbour businessman/Key 04. Ngong Ping alone awards Key 05. The short House mission requires all five physical keys and yields a separate 70-digit safe code before USB decryption. Dialogue, awareness, and ordinary temporary incapacitation cannot satisfy target objectives or grant keys. Target IDs, House code, and physical key IDs persist independently, and campaign completion requires explicit target records and mission prerequisites. F5/F9 use separate per-operation checkpoints. The USB’s contents and client remain unresolved.

Mission Selection also offers **independent exploration previews**; these do not change campaign progress. The campaign operations currently reuse the station, city and mountain blockouts. The three city assignments share the same street map, with distinct accessible interiors—not three finished districts, a 15× expansion or complete waterfront. The mountain field archive is outside the non-destructible public Buddha landmark. NPC routines are short and local; security can fire fictional short-stagger pulses, but there is no health, injury or advanced combat. See [HONG_KONG_MAP_PLAN.md](HONG_KONG_MAP_PLAN.md).

## Meridian expansion and interface
The original hotel coordinates and mission objectives remain. A connected west wing adds a dining/lounge area, upper guest rooms and an upstairs private suite for Locke. The two levels provide roughly seven times the original single-floor gross surface area, while the horizontal footprint is roughly 3.5 times larger; wall and stairwell space reduce actual walkable area. The ramp is a traversable incline, not animated stairs. This remains low-poly, with incomplete room detail, some visual-only furniture and an NPC navigation mesh that does not connect floors. Upper-floor save validation is approximate. The target follows a short suite-local routine; original NPCs remain on the original floor.

The HUD uses contextual prompts, top-centre objectives, local awareness and equipment/charges. No health or omniscient radar was invented. Documents and the archive/sensor terminal use original procedural geometry; some assessment and exit markers remain abstract. Player acceleration, visual turning, gait and footstep cadence were adjusted, but motion and camera feel need human assessment.

## Status

This remains a stylized prototype, **not an AAA production**. The Meridian now has a reception, lounge, wayfinding, corridor trim, artwork and workspaces; the annex has cooler workstations and assessment-lane dressing. Much of this remains simple, non-colliding procedural geometry over the original layouts. The Ruby scene has an original low-poly exterior, sky, opening doors, characters, subtitles and synthesized tones, not voice acting or polished authored animation.
